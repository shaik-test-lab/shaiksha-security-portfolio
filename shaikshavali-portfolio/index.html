  • Support security audits and governance risk assessments by reviewing control evidence, identifying gaps, and sharing findings and recommended actions with stakeholders.
  • Design and assess security controls, with periodic reviews of design and operating effectiveness across applications.
  • Review security policies, procedures and process documents against regulatory and statutory requirements, and maintain documentation and controls for multiple application environments.
  • Assess information security practices for data confidentiality, integrity and availability, and contribute to security, privacy and compliance measures.
  • Keep working knowledge of perimeter and endpoint controls, including firewalls, IDS/IPS, EDR and XDR, and run security audits to find vulnerabilities and control weaknesses.
  • Document audit findings, control gaps, corrective actions and risk register updates. Monitor policy adherence and track incidents and workflow tickets in Jira.

How I run an audit

The same five steps apply whether the scope is one application or a full process review.

  1. 1

    Collect evidence

    Gather policies, records and system evidence for the controls in scope.

  2. 2

    Test controls

    Check design and operating effectiveness against ISO 27001:2022 and ITGC expectations.

  3. 3

    Document findings

    Record each control gap with what the evidence showed and the risk it creates.

  4. 4

    Recommend action

    Agree corrective and preventive actions with the people who own the control.

  5. 5

    Track to closure

    Update the risk register and follow remediation in Jira until the gap is closed.
